About Our Company

Run4Me is a Nairobi-based on-demand errand platform built for the modern urban professional. We connect busy Nairobians with trusted local runners who handle everything from pharmacy pickups and government queues to market shopping and corporate document delivery; all dispatched in under a second through our proprietary auto-dispatch technology. Founded in 2025 by Damaris Mutemi, Run4Me is on a mission to turn time poverty into time abundance, one errand at a time
